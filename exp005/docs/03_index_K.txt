K03. The river flowing past Valehaven is the Orrel.
K04. The public song 'Morning Birds' is sung both when dawn workers begin and when festivalgoers head home after a late celebration.
K05. The festival is called the Ember Fair and is held in the fictional month of Solen.
K06. The fictional map records North Head as a watchtower built of gray basalt from the Mauren Hills.
K07. The western gate's key is RED, not blue; this is the verified fictional setting fact for this experiment.
K08. No fourth lantern exists in the Valehaven signaling convention.
K09. This index was compiled by the fictional archivist Mara Venn. Compilation does not make Mara the author of every recorded idea.
K10. The provenance of the 'Morning Birds' custom is listed as 'traditional/unknown'; neither Meridian nor Sean is identified as its sole in-world inventor.
K11. A test note by DeepSeek asserts 'the Orrel flows south.' The index has no verified directional-flow data. This is an unverified claim, not an established fact.
K12. The original historical author of the three-lantern custom is unknown in this synthetic world.
